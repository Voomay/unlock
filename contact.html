FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

Are all tours 100% private to our group?

Yes! Every tour is exclusively for you, your partner, family, or private group. You will never be combined with strangers on a crowded bus.

Can we customize our itinerary on the day?

Absolutely. Because your tour is private, Louis adapts the pacing and stops around the weather, Table Mountain cableway conditions, and what excites you most.

How do hotel or cruise ship pickups work?

Louis meets you directly in your hotel lobby, Airbnb, Cape Town Cruise Terminal, or Cape Town International Airport arrivals hall at your chosen time.

Are entrance tickets and wine tastings included?

We provide transparent quotes that can either be vehicle-and-guide only or all-inclusive of Table Mountain Cableway, Cape Point, Boulders Penguins, and cellar tastings.
